# Part 2: Judges' Q&A Cheat Sheet (Top 8 Tough Questions)

---

### Q1: "Why did you choose D-PAD navigation instead of free cursor eye-tracking?"
**Your Answer:**
> *"Continuous cursor eye-tracking sounds high-tech on paper, but in clinical reality, it is exhausting. Patients with Cerebral Palsy or ALS suffer from involuntary muscle spasms and tremors, which makes holding a tiny cursor steady nearly impossible and causes eye fatigue within 10 minutes.
> 
> Our discrete D-PAD uses an **Exponential Moving Average (EMA) filter** that cancels out tremors. It turns navigation into predictable, low-fatigue directional steps (`UP`, `DOWN`, `LEFT`, `RIGHT`) that anyone can operate for hours without fatigue."*

---

### Q2: "Why open the mouth to confirm instead of dwelling (holding gaze for 2 seconds)?"
**Your Answer:**
> *"Dwell timers are notoriously frustrating for motor-impaired individuals. If a patient has an involuntary spasm at second 1.8, the timer resets, or worse, triggers the wrong item. 
> 
> Opening the jaw is controlled by the cranial trigeminal motor pathway, which often remains intact even in severe quadriplegia. It is a distinct, voluntary gesture. Combining 'head aim + mouth open' gives the user 100% intentional control with zero delay."*

---

### Q3: "What if the room is dark or lighting is poor?"
**Your Answer:**
> *"On smartphones, Google ML Kit operates using adaptive neural contrast edge-detection which handles standard indoor ambient lighting.
> 
> However, on our target commercial hardware — **AI Smart Glasses** — the problem disappears completely. Smart glasses utilize **inward-facing Infrared (IR) micro-cameras and micro-IMUs**, which illuminate the eyes and face with invisible IR light, providing 100% tracking accuracy in pitch-black rooms."*

---

### Q4: "Why should Telekom Malaysia be the hardware distributor instead of just offering an app download?"
**Your Answer:**
> *"If we only sell software, users are forced to buy grey-market glasses from Shopee or Lazada. Those devices have uncertified lithium batteries, zero warranty, and require complicated technical sideloading that elderly caregivers cannot do.
> 
> TM already has the infrastructure: nationwide TM Point service centers, verified procurement, and existing installment billing. TM can offer **RM 0 upfront, 1-to-1 swap warranty, and out-of-the-box pre-configured devices**, creating a trusted brand moat that grey-market sellers cannot touch."*

---

### Q5: "How does the Bluetooth control work without installing anything on the TV or PC?"
**Your Answer:**
> *"We use native **Bluetooth HID (Human Interface Device) Device Profile**, implemented directly using standard USB HID specifications (Report ID 8).
> 
> To the Smart TV (Unifi TV Box, Android TV, Samsung Tizen) or laptop (Windows, Mac), our system identifies itself as a standard physical Bluetooth wireless keyboard. It requires **zero host software, zero drivers, and zero Wi-Fi network**. It pairs out-of-the-box just like an ordinary Logitech keyboard."*

---

### Q6: "Why did you design '5 Gazes to the Left' to switch into AAC mode?"
**Your Answer:**
> *"Safety and intentionality. If a patient is watching Unifi TV or Netflix, a simple single glance shouldn't disrupt their show. 
> 
> But when an emergency happens, or when they need water or the toilet, they need to switch modes **without asking anyone for help**. 5 rapid deliberate glances to the left acts as a reliable 'hands-free secret handshake' that cannot be triggered by accident."*

---

### Q7: "How does this benefit TM employees specifically?"
**Your Answer:**
> *"TM employs over 19,000 Malaysians. Many staff members have elderly parents recovering from strokes or family members with physical disabilities. 
> 
> Offering LAMIA as an internal **TM Employee Welfare Benefit** lightens the emotional and financial caregiving burden of TM's own workforce. It proves that TM takes care of its own family first, which boosts internal employee morale and retention while generating authentic social impact stories."*

---

### Q8: "How does TM save on corporate taxes through this project?"
**Your Answer:**
> *"Under Malaysian tax law (Income Tax Act Section 34(6)(h)), approved Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) expenditures and community assistive programs are eligible for **tax deductions**.
> 
> Furthermore, projects advancing national digital inclusion qualify for **MDEC and MyDIGITAL incentives**. By channeling CSR budgets through Yayasan TM into subsidized LAMIA device allocations for OKU communities, TM creates measurable social impact while legitimately optimizing corporate tax liability."*
