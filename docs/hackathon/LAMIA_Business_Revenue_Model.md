# LAMIA — Business & Revenue Model
### AI Smart Glasses That Give Voice to Those Who Can't Speak

---

## The Problem

Millions of people with Cerebral Palsy, stroke, ALS, or bedridden conditions **cannot speak or move their hands**. Their caregivers guess what they need — water? food? toilet? — through painful trial and error. Both sides end up frustrated and exhausted.

Today's assistive devices (like medical eye-trackers) cost **RM 20,000 – RM 50,000**. Most families simply cannot afford them.

---

## The Solution: LAMIA

LAMIA is software that runs inside **AI Smart Glasses** (like RayNeo, Rokid, or XREAL). It uses the built-in camera to read the user's **head movements and mouth gestures** to:

1. **Control a Smart TV or Laptop** — navigate channels, apps, and menus using simple head tilts, no hands needed.
2. **Call for help instantly** — the user looks at one of 4 big buttons (EAT, WATER, TOILET, HELP) and opens their mouth to confirm. The glasses speak out loud to alert the caregiver nearby.

No expensive hospital equipment. No complicated setup. Just put on the glasses and go.

## How TM Makes Money (4 Revenue Streams)

### 1. Monthly Subscription Bundle (B2C)
* Families subscribe through their **Unifi bill** — just like a phone installment plan.
* **RM 59 – RM 89 / month** for 24 months. Includes authentic AI Smart Glasses + LAMIA software.
* **RM 0 upfront** — families pay nothing to start.

### 2. Caregiver Cloud Add-On (SaaS)
* **RM 19 – RM 29 / month** for premium features:
  * Auto-send WhatsApp/SMS alerts to family when "HELP" is triggered.
  * Daily activity dashboard (what the user asked for, how often, patterns).
  * Extra voice packs (Bahasa Melayu, English, Mandarin, Tamil).

### 3. Hospital & Nursing Home Fleets (B2B via TM One)
* TM One leases LAMIA glasses to hospitals and rehab centers.
* **RM 150 – RM 250 per bed / month** — managed service with replacements and training included.
* Target: KPJ, Sunway Medical, IJN, government rehab hospitals, nursing homes.

### 4. Government Grants & Subsidies (B2G)
* Partner with JKM (Jabatan Kebajikan Masyarakat) and Yayasan TM.
* Government funds subsidized glasses for registered OKU cardholders.
* TM earns distribution and service revenue from each unit deployed.

## Sole Distributor Advantage (Why Not Shopee?)

Cheap, unbranded smart glasses flood Shopee and Lazada from unknown Chinese sellers. Families face:
* No warranty, no local support, no returns.
* Uncertified batteries and questionable safety.
* No pre-installed assistive software — useless out of the box.

**TM as the sole authorized distributor** solves all of this:
* ✅ 100% authentic, certified hardware.
* ✅ Pre-loaded with LAMIA, ready to use immediately.
* ✅ Full warranty and swap service at any TM Point nationwide.
* ✅ Monthly billing on Unifi — no large upfront cash needed.

## Social Impact & Tax Benefits

### CSR That Pays Back
Under Malaysian tax law, companies receive **tax deductions for Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) spending**. By funding LAMIA deployments to OKU communities, TM can:
* **Reduce taxable income** through eligible CSR deductions.
* **Claim incentives under MDEC and MyDIGITAL** initiatives for digital inclusion projects.
* Partner with **Yayasan TM** to channel CSR budgets into measurable, life-changing outcomes.

### TM Employee Welfare
Some of TM's own staff have parents, children, or siblings living with disabilities. Offering LAMIA as an **employee benefit program** sends a powerful message:
> *"We take care of our own people first."*

This boosts employee loyalty, morale, and reinforces TM as a compassionate employer — a story the media and public will amplify organically.

### National & Global Recognition
* LAMIA positions TM as a **social impact leader** in Southeast Asia — not just a telco selling bandwidth.
* Awards eligibility: UN SDG Awards, ASEAN ICT Awards, Prime Minister's CSR Award, Frost & Sullivan Best Practices.
